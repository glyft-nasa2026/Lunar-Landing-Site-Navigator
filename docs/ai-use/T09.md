## Choices the AI made that the task doesn't spell out

- **Which value is best.** Higher is better for Sun now (share of disc), Earth now, lit %, longest light and Earth contact %. Lower is better for longest dark and longest gap. For the two countdown rows: while up, a later set is better ("never sets" best); while down, an earlier rise is better ("never rises" or "permanently shadowed" worst). Nothing is marked with one column, or when every column is the same.
- **Strip edges.** The samples are hourly, so a bar edge would be up to an hour off the countdown. The first edge (the next sunset, or the next sunrise) is moved to the exact `flipFor` time, so the bar ends where "Sunset in" says. Later edges keep the one-hour step.
- **Redraws.** Pinned scenarios never change, so the module redraws only when the pinned list or the data changes, not on every live edit or timeline drag.
- On a phone the table scrolls sideways inside its own box; the page does not.
