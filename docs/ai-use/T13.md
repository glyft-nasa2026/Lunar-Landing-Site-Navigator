## Choices the AI made that the task doesn't spell out

- **"Meaningful" is 6 hours or more** of sunlight or Earth contact over the 30 days, or of shift in the next rise or set: under 1 % of the window. On a smooth Moon a 1 km move tilts the horizon by 0.03 degrees, and Earth, which crosses the horizon at about half a degree a day, then rises an hour or two earlier or later. That is real but small; the countdown table still shows it.
- **Rises and sets are compared as moments in time**, not as countdowns. So ±1 day reports no change in the words (it is the same sunset, seen from a day nearer), while the table shows both countdowns 24 h apart.
- **Each nudge starts from the live scenario**, not from the last ghost. To go 2 km, press Keep and nudge again. Pressing the same button again takes the ghost away.
- **The ghost goes away by itself** when the live scenario changes some other way (map click, time box, timeline), since it no longer means "one step from here".
- ±1 day is disabled when it would leave the Sun and Earth table.
