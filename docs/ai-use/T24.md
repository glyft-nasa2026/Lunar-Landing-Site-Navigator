## Limitations

- The timing depends on the machine and on what else runs: the same skyline has measured from about 40 ms to over 100 ms. The test passes on the fastest of five warm calls; a very busy machine can still fail it with the code unchanged. T05's own synthetic-grid timing test was raised to 150 ms for the same reason (issue #67); this check keeps T24's 100 ms.
- "Lit" uses the contract's model: the Sun's disc against the 240 m skyline from 2 m eye height, no scattered light, no terrain finer than 240 m. The rim percentage is our project calculation, not a figure from the paper the point comes from (Gläser et al. 2018 give 85.5 % on their 20 m map over twenty years).
- The two full-table checks take about 1.5 to 2 s each; they have a 300 s timeout so a slow CI machine does not cut them off.
